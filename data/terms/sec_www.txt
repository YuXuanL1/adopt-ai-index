Reviewed source: SEC EDGAR access policy
Review date: 2026-11-05
Official policy: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data

Use for this project: SEC EDGAR filings are public and may be accessed through the
SEC's official download routes. The project will not crawl the EDGAR HTML interface
when an API or bulk-data route is available.

Collection conditions noted in the SEC policy:
- Current maximum request rate: 10 requests per second.
- Use efficient scripting; download only what is needed and minimize server load.
- Send a declared User-Agent header that identifies the requester and contact email.
- Do not use botnets or other automated tools outside the SEC's acceptable policy.

Project decision proposed: use the official SEC APIs/bulk downloads at 5 requests per
second or less, with the declared team User-Agent. Preserve filing URLs and dates for citation.
