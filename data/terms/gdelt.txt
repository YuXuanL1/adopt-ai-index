Official policy: https://gdeltproject.org/about.html

Use for this project: GDELT may be used to discover relevant news coverage and public
article links. It is supplemental context, not the primary evidence for a firm's score.

Conditions noted in the GDELT terms:
- GDELT describes its released datasets as available for unrestricted academic use.
- Use or redistribution must cite the GDELT Project and link to its website.
- Keep the original article URL/date and verify material claims against the article or
  a primary source before using them in the index.

Project decision proposed: approved for API-based discovery with GDELT attribution.
