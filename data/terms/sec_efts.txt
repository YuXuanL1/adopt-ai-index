Reviewed source: SEC EDGAR full-text search access policy
Review date: 2026-11-05
Official policy: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data

Use for this project: the SEC full-text search service is a discovery tool for public
EDGAR filings. Any resulting evidence will be tied back to the original filing URL.

Collection conditions noted in the SEC policy:
- Follow fair-access practices, including no more than 10 requests per second.
- Use efficient, limited requests and a declared User-Agent with contact information.
- Do not use automated collection outside the SEC's acceptable policy.

Project decision proposed: approved for limited API discovery under the same controls
as the SEC filings APIs.
